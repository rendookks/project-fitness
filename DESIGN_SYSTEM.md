# Design system

Premium athletic technology with calm surfaces, generous whitespace, and strong numerical hierarchy.

- Background `#090D16`, secondary `#111827`, elevated `#1C2535`, border `#293345`.
- Text `#F6F8FC`, secondary `#A3AFC2`.
- Iris `#A5A3FF`: primary actions and navigation. Aqua `#80DED8`: supporting analytics. Champagne `#F2C39A`: records and achievements.
- Manrope (Google Fonts, SIL Open Font License 1.1), locally bundled. Display 36, large 30, screen 26, section 20, body 16, secondary 14, caption 12. Tabular numerals where supported. Font scaling stays enabled.
- Spacing 4/8/12/16/24/32/48; screen inset 20. Radii 10/16/24.
- Touch targets at least 44 points, labeled icons, safe areas, keyboard avoidance, visible loading/error/empty states. No animation required to save.
- Today emphasizes one workout action; Train favors large numeric controls; Progress uses readable labeled charts. Fuel, Learn, Recovery clearly disclose their informational status.

Final visual approval and native text/keyboard behavior require founder review on phone.
